# Hollowmere lore (working draft)

The story Death Muffin is aiming at. Nothing here is in the game yet unless it says so; it guides names, dialogue, art and what the
classes and bosses are about. Status of each item: **Settled** (decided, build on it), **Shelved** (set aside on purpose, do not build
it, may return), **Later** (wanted, not now), **Open** (a question nobody has answered; do not invent an answer in game text).
Decided in the team's job board thread, 2026-10-27. The full draft was posted in the "Current Lore" post.

## Premise (Settled)

The Covenant's burial bell was cursed and broke. Its toll used to carry the dead onward; now souls are trapped and the dead are restless.

## The seven keepers (Settled)

One keeper per rite stage, the same seven bosses as in `client/data/content/bosses.json`:

1. The Gravedigger King
2. The Bone Abbess
3. The Drowned Congregation
4. The Plague Saint
5. The Cinder Regent
6. The Mire Mother
7. The Bell-Sworn Prelate

Each was consumed by their vows. They keep their duty to the Covenant but can no longer tell the living from the dead.

## Classes

- **Settled:** Hollow Knight, Carrion Witch, Grave Warden, Monk.
- **Shelved:** Veilwalker, Reaper. Both wait on what happens to freed souls (see Open questions).
- **Later:** Ossuary, Gravecaller, Mourner, Rotweaver.

## Tone and look (Settled)

Hollow Knight-style sparse sadness: few words, quiet grief, no speeches. The look is autumn-gothic with a Halloween feel.

## Zones (Settled as a story layout)

Forest, churchyard, sanctuary, crypt, town. Whether this matches the game's real areas is still open (below).

## Shelved

- The necromancer lord villain. The tragedy is the keepers and the broken bell, not a single evil mastermind.

## Open questions

- Who or what cursed the bell, and what part the Prelate played.
- What happens to freed souls. Answering this unblocks Veilwalker and Reaper.
- Whether the Prior, Sexton and Apothecary dialogue fits this lore.
- The Monk's design. It is a full rework and needs its own job.
- Whether it is always autumn or the season varies by area.
- Whether the zone layout above matches the game's real areas.

## Not in the game today

A world boss, a hub town, and any seasonal event. Ideas for them are one-liners in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) "Ideas".
