# Crossworlds audio accents (removed)

Three short recordings (`Dark Magic Spell 1`, `Dark Magic Spell 2`, `Magic Cast
Whoosh 2-1`) were once extracted from the local Crossworlds Unity client archive
(`sharedassets0.assets`) and shipped as `public/audio/crossworlds/*.ogg`. The archive
had no audio licence or attribution file, so their redistribution rights were never
confirmed.

On 2026-10-02 the combat audio pass removed them and replaced each with a CC0
equivalent built from Kenney packs (Litany, Exhume and Veil rite layers); see
[AUDIO-SOURCES.md](AUDIO-SOURCES.md). Do not re-add recordings of unclear origin.
