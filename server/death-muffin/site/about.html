The game

Death is the resource.

Death Muffin is an overhead action RPG for Windows, played online. You install it with the Death Muffin launcher, which also keeps it up to date. The Ossuary Covenant holds a safe chapterhouse and garden at the top of a long descent, and everything below it is full of the dead. Kill what lives there. Take what it drops. Raise what remains.

There are no extraction timers or mission resets, and you play solo unless you make a party. Keep farming, shape your build, open the sealed passages one by one, and decide how much pressure you can survive.

The pressure

Control how fast the dead arrive.

Every kill feeds your next decision. Equip stronger drops, buy more damage, or speed up the waves. Faster waves mean denser fights, greater risk, and richer rewards. Then, when you have buried the Bell-Sworn Prelate, swear Vows at the Altar of Ascension.

Each Vow is a curse you choose: older dead, no flasks, a Prelate who learns new tricks. The heat you swear is your rank, and your seals never reset. The difficulty dial belongs to the player.

Your covenant

An army made from yesterday’s enemies.

Four necromancers raise the dead, and they are the disciplines you can play now: Ossuary, Gravecaller, Mourner and Rotweaver. Five New Blood disciplines (Grave Warden, Bell Monk, Carrion Witch, Hollow Knight, Veilwalker) fight them with oil, bells, hooks, oaths and the veil, and they come later. Every body can become power—but never in every form at once.

  • Raise thralls from fresh corpses, and give them spare weapons and armour.
  • Harvest bodies for Grave Essence, Oil, Offal or Resonance, whatever your discipline runs on.
  • Equip five-piece armour sets, socket relic runes into your rites and swap whole loadouts in one click.

The descent

Thirteen places, each deeper than the last.

I

The Chapterhouse

A safe hub with the Reliquary, the Workbench, the Altar of Ascension and a Waystone.

II

The Sexton’s Acre

A peaceful yard for gathering, a garden, laborers and daily contracts.

III

The Alchemist’s Wing

The Great Cauldron and the Apothecary, who tells you what your reagents make.

IV

The Hollow Graves

The first hunting ground, and the first king of the dead.

V

The Catacomb Warren

Nine chambers, and something digging in each. Opens after 150 Graves kills.

VI

The Marrow Ossuary

Narrow passages, dense packs and the Bone Abbess.

VII

The Drowned Nave

The congregation never stopped kneeling. Wraiths sing rings onto the floor.

VIII

The Bone Coliseum

The dead cheer for whoever is still standing.

IX

The Catacomb Depths

An endless stair of seeded floors, one level older each time, with chests as you go.

X

The Bell Sanctum

Seat of the Bell-Sworn Prelate, and the end of the first road.

XI

The Plague Cloister

Where the blight grows with you: its dead scale to your level.

XII

The Cinder Pyre

Burning ground, ash circles and the Cinder Regent.

XIII

The Mourning Fen

A drowned marsh of hummocks and bog water, and the Mire Mother.

The deeper the ground, the better its drops: higher item levels, more runes and a growing chance of legendary armour. Gear sets, relic runes and boss-only legendaries are all waiting for you down there.
