The Ossuary Covenant

Choose your discipline.

Every necromancer raises the dead. Your discipline decides whether those bodies become a shield wall, a restless legion, a choir of wraiths, or a garden of rot.

Four ways to spend the dead

The Covenant’s disciplines

I

Tank · Bone

Ossuary

Keeper of the Bone Wall

Raise a wall of Shieldbearers, stand behind it, and turn every sacrifice into armour.

Passive · Bone Ward

Thralls gain 100% health and draw aggression. You take 10% less damage per active thrall. Black Litany grants a bone barrier.

II

Army · Spirit

Gravecaller

Marshal of the Restless

Raise, sacrifice, and raise again. Your corpse field never stays empty for long.

Passive · Grave Legion

Command up to five thralls. They attack 20% faster, and those sacrificed by Black Litany leave corpses behind.

III

Sustain · Wraith

Mourner

Singer of the Funeral Rite

Bind ranged spirits and recover through the very rites that consume your dead.

Passive · Funeral Rites

Exhume raises ranged Wraiths. Consuming a corpse heals 10% maximum health, and Grave Essence regenerates 25% faster.

IV

Control · Decay

Rotweaver

Gardener of Decay

Seed rot where enemies gather and make every corpse in the circle bloom violently.

Passive · Carrion Bloom

Miasma Circle is 40% wider and Withered stacks to eight. Corpses inside Miasma burst, damaging and withering nearby enemies.
