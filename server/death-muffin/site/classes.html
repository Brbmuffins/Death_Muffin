The Ossuary Covenant

Choose your discipline.

Nine disciplines walk the Covenant's halls. Four are necromancers who raise the dead as a shield wall, a restless legion, a choir of wraiths, or a garden of rot. Five are the New Blood, who fight the dead with oil, bells, hooks, oaths and the veil.

Nine ways to meet the dead

The Covenant’s disciplines

I

Tank · Bone

Ossuary

Keeper of the Bone Wall

Raise a wall of Shieldbearers, stand behind it, and turn every sacrifice into armour.

Passive · Bone Ward

Thralls gain 100% health and draw aggression. You take 10% less damage per active thrall. Black Litany grants a bone barrier.

II

Army · Spirit

Gravecaller

Marshal of the Restless

Raise, sacrifice, and raise again. Your corpse field never stays empty for long.

Passive · Grave Legion

Command up to five thralls. They attack 20% faster, and those sacrificed by Black Litany leave corpses behind.

III

Sustain · Wraith

Mourner

Singer of the Funeral Rite

Bind ranged spirits and recover through the very rites that consume your dead.

Passive · Funeral Rites

Exhume raises ranged Wraiths. Consuming a corpse heals 10% maximum health, and Grave Essence regenerates 25% faster.

IV

Control · Decay

Rotweaver

Gardener of Decay

Seed rot where enemies gather and make every corpse in the circle bloom violently.

Passive · Carrion Bloom

Miasma Circle is 40% wider and Withered stacks to eight. Corpses inside Miasma burst, damaging and withering nearby enemies.

V

Support · Oil

Grave Warden

Keeper of the Last Lantern

Burn corpses for Oil, pin enemies with a chain and shelter allies in lantern light.

Passive · Lamplighter

Oil refills steadily and rises when a corpse burns. Lantern wards protect everyone standing inside.

VI

Rhythm · Resonance

Bell Monk

Voice of the Last Toll

Strike on a twelve-beat rhythm, build Resonance and turn corpses into sounding bells.

Passive · The Beat

Hits close to each toll deal more damage and build Resonance faster.

VII

Hexes · Offal

Carrion Witch

Mistress of Hooks and Crows

Harvest corpses for Offal, send crows into a pack and curse foes with a hooked charm.

Passive · Corpse Hunger

Offal comes from bodies, never passive regeneration. Save a corpse before spending it.

VIII

Bruiser · Rage

Hollow Knight

Oathbound of the Covenant

Build Rage by taking and dealing punishment, then spend it leaping into the dead.

Passive · Oathbound

Rage instead of Grave Essence: built by damage taken, by every body Hollow Cut catches, and fastest of all by a perfect block. It drains once you leave the fight.

IX

Mobility · Veil

Veilwalker

One Foot Beyond

Walk between life and death and raise brief spectral allies from echoes of the fallen.

Passive · Thin Places

Veil refills in Life form and drains in Veil form. Spectral echoes appear where corpses fall.

Shared by every discipline

The necromancer’s kit.

The four necromancers share one pool of rites, and your discipline changes the economy around them—not the spells themselves. You choose five unlocked rites for keys 1 to 5, socket relic runes into them, and can save whole loadouts. The New Blood each bring a kit of their own, built on a different resource.
